# NextStage — Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Name & Positioning

**Name:** NextStage
**Category:** AI-powered SaaS for independent artists
**Positioning statement:** NextStage helps independent artists discover the venues, festivals, and booking opportunities that actually fit their sound — using AI to turn scattered scene data into a ranked, actionable path forward.

**Core brand ideas the identity must express:**
- Progression / "the next step"
- Live music and booking, without literal instruments or mics
- Discovery and intelligence (AI-driven matching)
- Momentum — forward, upward motion

**Brand personality:** confident, credible, modern, quietly technical. Not playful/startup-cute; not corporate-cold. Feels like a serious tool a touring artist or their manager would trust.

---

## 2. Logo Usage Rules

- The wordmark is always set in **Manrope, weight 800 (ExtraBold)**, letter-spacing **-0.02em to -0.03em**.
- The mark and wordmark maintain a fixed ratio: icon height ≈ wordmark cap-height × 1.68 (e.g. 64px icon next to 38px wordmark).
- Never redraw, rotate, skew, or recolor the arrow-N geometry.
- Never separate the icon from the wordmark in the horizontal lockup except when using the compact icon variant intentionally (app icons, favicons, avatar contexts).
- Maintain the curved diagonal stroke and rounded stroke caps/joins in every reproduction — the mark is deliberately organic, not sharp.
- Do not place the gradient version at sizes below 32px — switch to the monochrome or solid-color version instead (gradients degrade at small sizes).

## 3. Logo Variants

1. **Horizontal lockup — light**: gradient icon + dark wordmark (`#12101F`) on white/light backgrounds.
2. **Horizontal lockup — dark**: gradient icon + light wordmark (`#FAFAFC`) on dark backgrounds (`#0B0A10` / `#0E0D1A`).
3. **Compact icon**: icon only, used as app icon / avatar / social profile image. Comes in light-background and dark-tile forms.
4. **Monochrome**: single-color icon + wordmark, in all-dark (`#12101F`) or all-light (`#FAFAFC`), for single-color print, embossing, or low-contrast contexts.
5. **Favicon-friendly**: icon on a rounded-square gradient tile (white glyph), optimized and stroke-thickened at 16 / 32 / 64px.

## 4. Clear Space & Minimum Size

- **Clear space:** maintain a minimum margin around the full lockup equal to the height of the arrowhead in the mark (≈ 1 icon-unit at the rendered size). No text, edges, or other logos may enter this space.
- **Minimum size, horizontal lockup:** 120px wide (digital), 1 inch wide (print).
- **Minimum size, compact icon:** 24px (digital), 0.25 inch (print).
- **Favicon minimum:** 16px — use the favicon-specific asset (heavier stroke weight) below 32px, never the standard icon scaled down.

## 5. Approved Backgrounds

- Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) — light lockup.
- App near-black (`#0B0A10`) or dark surface (`#0E0D1A` / `#16151D`) — dark lockup.
- Light neutral canvas (`#EEEEF2`) — light lockup, e.g. printed materials.
- Do not place the logo on busy photography, low-contrast mid-tones, or on top of the brand gradient itself.

## 6. Incorrect Logo Usage

Do not:
- Stretch, squash, or change the aspect ratio of the icon or wordmark.
- Change the wordmark font, weight, or tracking.
- Apply drop shadows, bevels, outlines, or additional effects.
- Recolor the gradient to non-brand hues.
- Sharpen the corners of the arrowhead or straighten the N's curved diagonal.
- Place the gradient lockup on a busy or low-contrast background.
- Rebuild the arrow or N from a different typeface's letterform.

## 7. Brand Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Brand gradient start | `#4B4DF7` | Indigo — gradient stop 0% |
| Brand gradient mid | `#9333EA` | Violet — gradient stop 50%, solid accent |
| Brand gradient end | `#F43F5E` | Pink/rose — gradient stop 100% |
| Dark lockup gradient start | `#6D6BFF` | Indigo (light-adjusted for dark bg) |
| Dark lockup gradient mid | `#A855F7` | Violet (light-adjusted) |
| Dark lockup gradient end | `#FB5B76` | Pink (light-adjusted) |
| Accent (solid) | `#9333EA` | Primary interactive accent, matches logo |
| Accent (on-dark text/icon) | `#C084FC` | Accent text/icons on dark surfaces |
| Ink / dark neutral | `#12101F` | Wordmark on light, monochrome dark |
| Paper / light neutral | `#FAFAFC` | Wordmark on dark, monochrome light |
| App background | `#0B0A10` | Darkest app surface |
| Surface | `#16151D` | Card / panel background |
| Surface raised | `#1D1B26` | Active/selected panel state |

The full gradient (`#4B4DF7 → #9333EA → #F43F5E`, 135°) is reserved for the logo mark and primary CTA buttons only — never used as a full-bleed background or decorative wash.

## 8. Visual Tone

- Dark, near-black UI surfaces with restrained use of color.
- Purple/violet is the single accent — used sparingly, for key actions, active states, and highlights only.
- No neumorphism, no heavy glassmorphism, no busy gradients outside the logo/CTA.
- Generous negative space; confident, large type; flat surfaces over skeuomorphic depth.

## 9. Imagery Direction

- Avoid literal, generic music iconography (microphones, music notes, waveforms as decoration).
- Where photography is used, favor real venue/stage/crowd photography with a sense of scale and forward motion, desaturated toward the dark neutral palette, never full-color/saturated over-the-top concert photos.
- Abstract data/network visuals are acceptable to represent "intelligence" but should stay geometric and restrained — not sci-fi or neon.

## 10. Iconography Direction

- Simple, geometric, 2px-stroke line icons (rounded joins) inside rounded-square tiles with a tinted background (e.g. `rgba(147,51,234,0.14)` tile + solid accent stroke).
- No filled/glyph-heavy icon sets, no emoji, no literal instrument icons.
- Icon tile pattern: 34–44px rounded-square container, 10px radius, icon centered at ~40–50% of tile size.
